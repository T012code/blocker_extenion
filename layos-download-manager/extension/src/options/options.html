Pause and continue

Open the toolbar pop-up to see your downloads with their progress, and pause or continue them. Most servers let a download continue where it stopped, even after closing the app or restarting the computer. When a server doesn't, the download is marked Can't resume, and continuing it starts the file again from the beginning.
