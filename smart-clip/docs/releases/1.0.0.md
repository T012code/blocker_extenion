The first version of Smart Clip: your clipboard on all your PCs.

- Sign in with your email and a code sent to it, on as many Windows PCs as you like.
- Text, links and images you copy show up on your other PCs in a moment.
- A floating Smart Clip icon in the corner of any text box: click it, pick a clip, and it's pasted.
- Alt+Shift+V opens your clips at the text cursor in any app.
- Text clips last 7 days, images 24 hours. Passwords from password managers are never synced.
- Starts with Windows, runs quietly in the tray, light and dark mode.
