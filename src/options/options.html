      About

      Blocking happens inside the browser using its built-in request filtering, so pages load faster and nothing you browse is sent anywhere. This extension has no servers and collects no data.

      Block lists come from EasyList and EasyPrivacy, AdGuard filters, uBlock Origin's badware list and the NoCoin list. Thanks to everyone who maintains them.
